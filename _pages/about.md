I am a PhD student in applied mathematics at the University of Delaware, advised by Tobin Driscoll. I work on inverse problems for differential equations: given indirect, noisy, or partial observations of a physical system, what can be recovered about the parameters and dynamics that produced them? I approach these with neural operators, diffusion models, and simulation-based inference, on problems ranging from tear film breakup to chaotic PDE dynamics.

I expect to complete my PhD in June 2027 and am looking for postdoctoral positions. Instructor of record for nine courses at Delaware, plus six as a teaching assistant.
