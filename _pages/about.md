I am a PhD student in applied mathematics at the University of Delaware, advised by Tobin Driscoll, where I am also completing a concurrent MS in Data Science. I use machine learning to solve differential equations: neural operators, diffusion models, and simulation-based inference as alternatives to classical numerical schemes, applied to problems ranging from tear film breakup to chaotic PDE dynamics. Diffusion models run through most of this work — generating solution states, recovering model parameters, and completing sparse or missing observations.

Alongside my research, I have taught nine courses at Delaware as instructor of record and six more as a teaching assistant.

I expect to complete my PhD in June 2027 and am looking for postdoctoral positions.
